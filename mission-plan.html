What we've been asked to do, the three principles we're working from, what we're aiming at this year, and how we'll actually run it.

The charge

Prophets have clearly and powerfully taught us the importance of gathering Israel on both sides of the veil. President Russell M. Nelson called this the greatest work on earth today.

The Quorum of the Twelve Apostles has encouraged us to teach and practice normal and natural ways of sharing the gospel with those who are not members of the Church. They have also approved guiding principles to help members and missionaries in their efforts to gather Israel — and they have promised that when we apply those principles, they will allow the light of the gospel to shine brightly into every corner of the earth.

The three guiding principles

Approved by the Apostles. Read them as first person — they're written to be your words, not a description of someone else.

Love

“I love my Heavenly Father and His Son, Jesus Christ, and feel their love for me. I follow the Savior and strive to love my neighbor as He does. I want others to feel the love of my Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.”

Share

“As a faithful disciple of Jesus Christ, I let His light shine by serving others. I share my time, resources, testimony, and personal experiences to bless my friends and their families. I want them to feel the joy that comes from the Savior, His gospel, and His Church.”

Invite

“I pray for inspiration and courage to know how to invite my friends to ‘come and see’ and feel the goodness of the restored gospel and Church of Jesus Christ. I invite them to ‘come and help’ us serve God's children. I invite them to ‘come and belong’ to His Church.”

What we're aiming at

Identify individuals whom we feel are ready to progress along the covenant path, then evaluate progress quarterly. Our efforts aim to achieve the following.

  1. Help at least two individuals receive the restored gospel through faith in Jesus Christ, repentance, baptism, and receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost.
  2. Help at least four individuals become reactivated in gospel living — through faith in Jesus Christ and making, keeping, and renewing covenants with God through service to God's children, partaking of the sacrament, participation in church meetings, and temple attendance.
  3. Identify and cultivate new friendships with at least eight less-active members or non-members.
  4. Help all newly baptized members aged 11 and older attend the temple within 30 days of baptism.

How we'll do it

Love

  • Be intentional with ministering and fellowshipping assignments. This includes making assignments to all inactive and non-member individuals or families within our ward boundaries.

Share

  • Educate our ward members about this mission plan and the principles of love, share and invite — in ministering interviews, sacrament meeting talks, and second-hour discussions.
  • Encourage everyone to fulfill their assignments to minister and fellowship. Help ward members exercise faith and prayerfully seek and act on inspiration to reach out and share our time, service, friendship, testimonies and helpful life experiences with these individuals and families.

Invite

  • Invitations to reach out to all households should include church-sponsored ward and stake activities as well as community activities — service projects, neighborhood activities, and personal family activities.

Coordinate regularly

  • Weekly huddles to report and organize the daily efforts to love, share and invite.
    • Auxiliary representatives who attend will share huddle outcomes with their presidencies.
    • The ward council will provide direction and assignments to the ward missionary committee as they become aware of circumstances and needs.
    • Youth and Primary should be involved in carrying out these assignments, when possible.
    • Our stake's full-time missionaries should also be included, when possible.
  • In coordination between the ward council and the ward mission leader, maintain a list of about five families or individuals in need of special prayer and attention. Pray for those people by name and coordinate efforts to meet their temporal and spiritual needs. Adjust the list regularly as needed.

The other half of the same work

Gathering Israel happens on both sides of the veil. This plan is one side of it; the ward's temple and family history plan is the other, and they're meant to be run as one effort rather than two competing ones.
