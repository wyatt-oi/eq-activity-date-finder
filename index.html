Fine print before you ink these in

  • Gratitude Night is now Mon Nov 9, and it isn't a ward event. It moved off Wed Nov 11 because the stake has the ER7 combined youth activity that night, 4–9 p.m. Monday is family home evening, so the answer was to stop holding it at the building: we hand every family a short lesson and they run it at their own kitchen table. Nothing to schedule, nothing to compete with, and it reaches the families who never come out to an activity. Still lands two days before Veterans Day, so the veteran-and-family-stories framing holds.
  • Nebo District 2026–27 (official, tentative board approval): school begins Wed Aug 12 — the same day as Pickleball Night; fall break Oct 15–19; Thanksgiving Nov 25–27; Christmas break Dec 21–Jan 1; spring break Apr 5–9; last day Thu May 20, 2027 (half day) — the same day as AI Night. The 2027–28 calendar isn't published yet; fall 2027 school dates get added when it is.
  • Jazz schedule drops mid-August 2026 (season starts Tue Oct 20). Games will be added when the NBA releases the slate — mostly weeknights, so expect Thursday checks on AI Nights.
  • Basketball is partially mapped. BYU's non-conference is official (incl. Ohio State Nov 2, Maui over Thanksgiving, Northwestern Dec 5). Utah's is reported but unofficial until mid-August (Towson Nov 5, Weber St Nov 10, E. Washington Nov 17, Charleston Classic Thanksgiving week). Big 12 conference slates (Jan–Mar) release in the fall — January and March picks get re-checked then.
  • 2026 football kickoff times aren't announced yet. College Saturdays are blocked whole-day to be safe.
  • Fall 2027 college dates are TBD. Opponents are set (BYU hosts Utah in Provo) but dates release late fall 2026 — Sept–Nov 2027 Saturdays are blocked generically until then.
  • Stake calendar is merged in, at two levels. By default the day squares show only the items that can actually cost you a date — youth nights, leadership meetings that land on a Thursday, firesides, Camp Helaman, the Christmas devotional. The Stake calendar dropdown in the header opens up the other 83 events, either all at once or one bucket at a time: Leadership meetings (10 — stake council, high council), Baptisms & recommends (16), Youth & Primary (9 — mission-prep course, Primary program practice), YSA (22 — Gathering Place nights), Ward athletics (22 — 8th Ward men's basketball), Churchwide broadcasts (4). Those show as dots — hover or tap a day for the full list with times. Your pick is remembered, and the Church chip still filters both levels together.
  • Two things the default view leaves out on purpose. 8th Ward men's basketball holds the ward gym every Thursday 9–10:30 p.m. — late enough that it never touches a 7 p.m. activity, so marking every Thursday would just make clear nights look busy. Temple recommend interviews run 1st and 3rd Sundays 2–4 p.m. Both are one dropdown pick away — Ward athletics and Baptisms & recommends.
  • Still missing from the stake feed: stake conference, youth conference, girls camp, FSY, and temple closures — none are posted yet. Jan–May 2027 is only lightly filled in (youth nights and fifth-Sunday firesides), so winter and spring picks need a re-check once the stake publishes the rest.
  • March Madness dates are approximate until the NCAA publishes the 2027 bracket calendar.
